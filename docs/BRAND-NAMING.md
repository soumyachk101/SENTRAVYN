Exact quoted web searches returned no obvious exact-match websites for these three candidates in the checked results. **This does not establish that a name is unused, legally available, or that any domain is unregistered.** Search engines are incomplete and trademarks, mobile app stores, social handles and domain registries must be checked separately.

### Mandatory naming gate
- Check domain availability at an actual registrar for `.com`, `.org`, `.app` and appropriate regional TLDs.
- Check WIPO Global Brand Database and relevant national trademark registries (including USPTO and India IP India).
- Check Apple App Store, Google Play, GitHub, npm, social handles and broad web variants.
- Review pronunciation and unwanted meanings across major languages.
- Choose and purchase domain, then update `SITE_URL`, brand metadata, manifests, social images and legal pages.
- Until approval, keep `SENTRAVYN` as the internal codename. **Do not mass-rename files or deploy under a guessed domain.**
- Preserve all design and safety requirements regardless of final name.
