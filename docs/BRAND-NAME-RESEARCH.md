The owner selected SENTRAVYN as the project name. This is an approved project naming decision, not proof of legal or digital availability.

Before public launch or domain registration, independently verify:
- Exact and confusingly similar domains using registrar/RDAP/WHOIS services.
- WIPO Global Brand Database and relevant national trademark registers.
- Company and business registries in target markets where relevant.
- GitHub organisation/repository namespace.
- Apple App Store and Google Play app names.
- Social media handles.
- Existing products, businesses and common-law trademark use.

Record the date, query, jurisdiction, result and evidence. Consult qualified trademark counsel before commercial use where appropriate.

Do not claim trademark clearance, domain ownership or handle availability without evidence. Do not register or purchase anything without the owner's explicit approval.
